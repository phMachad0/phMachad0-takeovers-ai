# The corpus and the scope

## Layout on disk

```
data/<siren>/bilans/pdf/bilan_<data_deposito>_<doc_id>.pdf
data/<siren>/bilans/meta/bilan_<data_deposito>_<doc_id>.json    ← registry index
data/<siren>/bilans/ocr/<doc_id>/page_001.json                  ← OCR, 1 file per page
data/<siren>/actes/…                                            ← other challenge, ignore
```

The 24-hex-character `doc_id` is the INPI identifier and is the key that ties PDF, meta,
and OCR together.

## Notes per company

**`820561470` — SARL PAUTET.** The geometrically hardest: skew of −0.9 to −1.0 on most
pages, one cover page at 4.88°, and several near-empty pages (1 to 7 tokens) that are cover
scans. Capital of €10,000, fiscal year closing on 31/08.

**`328024377` — BERNACHON.** Chocolatier from Lyon. Controls `EURL BERNACHON PASSION` and
`SARL BERNACHON PARIS` (page 12 of the 2022 filing). Capital €152,500, 43 employees, fiscal
year closing on 30/06. This is the company behind the kEUR trap —
[[F002-the-keur-belongs-to-the-annexe-not-the-balance-sheet]].

**`445070311` — CHOCOLATERIE JACQUES BOCKEL.** Alsace. Capital €300,000, fiscal year
closing on 30/06. Plaquette produced by an accounting firm, with an audit report (Philippe
Hufschmitt, CRCC de Colmar). The 2023-11-21 filing has 58 pages — the largest in scope.

**`504304205`.** The only one with the full liasse, including 2054 through 2059-G. It is
the only document in scope where `META_AVG_WORKFORCE` sits in its canonical spot, the
**2058-C** (page 16 of the 2017 and 2018 filings).

**`401009741` — CREAMANDE.** SAS de capital variable, Vaucluse, fiscal year closing on
30/04. Clean liasse generated by Cegid. It's the easiest document and a good candidate to
develop the pipeline against first. **Watch out:** the 2025-10-03 filing
(`68f0a715f28d8aaf48046416`) only has 2050 and 2051 — there is no compte de résultat in
liasse form.

## The `meta/` files

Every PDF has a registry metadata JSON. **Not yet explored.** It may contain the fiscal
year closing date, which would save having to read it off the PDF to fill in
`fiscal_year_end`. **Pending:** inspect it.

## The rest of the corpus

There are 20 companies in `data/`. The other 15 belong to the *actes* challenge and should
be ignored — with one useful exception: if it becomes necessary to check whether any bilan
in the corpus is really in kEUR (see
[[F002-the-keur-belongs-to-the-annexe-not-the-balance-sheet]]), it's worth scanning all of
them.
