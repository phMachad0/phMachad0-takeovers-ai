## Conventions

- Findings carry a stable `F###` id and are never renumbered. Decisions carry `ADR-###`.
- Every factual claim about the corpus carries its evidence: file path, page, OCR text.
  Where there is none, the page says "not verified" explicitly.
- `log.md` is the chronological, append-only record. `index.md` is the by-topic catalogue.
